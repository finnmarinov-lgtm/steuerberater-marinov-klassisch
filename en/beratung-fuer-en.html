Our services are focused on international individuals

 

We offer tax consulting, social security assistance and support in business administration for

• foreign employees working in Germany as well as Germans working outside of Germany (Expatriates)
• international employees, capital investors, freelancers and entrepreneurs, real estate owners, retirees
• people who change their residence to another country

 

We also widely support German and foreign employers in planning and realizing international assignments.

We provide advice to employees, business founders, freelancers and small companies without international touch.

Consulting in Bulgarian language for Bulgarian citizens living in Germany is a fast growing part of our services.
